Ivory begins with 2 Tempo. Move a piece, place a No Go area, or turncoat an eligible enemy.

  • ■Cube
    Moves 1 tile. Turncoated by four occupied orthogonal or diagonal tiles forming a square, including at least one attacking piece.
  • ▲Wedge
    Moves through up to 3 connected tiles in any directions, may change direction and may jump pieces. Turncoating requires one of its four three-tile triangle formations.
  • ⬢Main Piece
    Completely surround the enemy Main Piece—including with occupied tiles and the red perimeter—to win.
  • ●Tempo
    Each action passes its Tempo cost to the opponent. If you begin with 3, spend at least 2 Tempo before passing; otherwise spend at least 1.
  • ×No Go
    Spend 2 Tempo to place one on any empty tile. It never moves, blocks movement and counts as a surrounding area.
  • ⬟Turncoat
    Spend 1 Tempo to convert an eligible surrounded enemy. Its blue shield prevents the opponent immediately converting it back.
  • ▦Gullies
    Optionally open ten spaces in the red boundary. They behave exactly like ordinary playable tiles.
Prototype assumptions

Pieces move horizontally, vertically or diagonally without jumping. The red perimeter and No Go areas are neutral blockers. To win, a complete surround must include one of your pieces and you must have 1 Tempo left to spend. Completing the surround around your own Main Piece forfeits the game.
